## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an editorial, academic, and dignified educational atmosphere tailored for structured online faith and character learning. Blending classic literary warmth with contemporary educational usability, the design communicates scholarly credibility, trust, spiritual calm, and approachability. 

The aesthetic leverages a refined balance of:
- **Literary Editorial Elegance:** Warm, contemplative parchment-like backgrounds paired with high-contrast, graceful serif headings evoking revered texts and classical scholarship.
- **Modern Institutional Utility:** Ultra-clean, highly legible sans-serif typography for body copy, data points, and interactive forms, preventing cognitive fatigue.
- **Deep Sanctuary Contrast:** Rich, obsidian-teal nocturnal tones for anchor elements (navigation and footer) contrasted against warm ivory canvas tiers, illuminated by vibrant emerald and spruce action elements.

The UI avoids sterile corporate minimalism or loud commercialism, grounding each interaction with quiet confidence, organic warmth, and structured clarity.

## Colors

The palette establishes an organic, literary warmth juxtaposed against authoritative nocturnal accents. 

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#0D7E6C`):** A disciplined, rich emerald spruce. Used for primary CTAs, icon fills, key milestones, and verified badges.
- **Secondary (`#072026`):** Deep abyssal slate-green. Used for foundational anchors such as global footers, deep hero banners, and high-impact editorial headings.
- **Tertiary (`#10B981`):** A lively jade accent used sparingly for micro-confirmations, active check indicators, and status points.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FBF9F4`):** Warm ivory parchment background that cushions eye strain and imparts a premium, classic editorial presence.
- **Neutral Support Surfaces (`#F4EFE6` & `#FFFFFF`):** Warm beige container washes for structured inset callouts, and pure crisp white for elevated utility cards (e.g., lead forms and pricing panels).
- **Text & Borders:** Headings use `#072026` for profound contrast, body copy uses soft slate `#374151`, and boundaries are delineated with muted golden-gray borders (`#E8E2D5`).

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy juxtaposes an authoritative, high-contrast serif with an ultra-pragmatic humanist sans-serif.

### Heading Hierarchy
- **Headline Font (`Playfair Display`):** Reserved for primary storytelling, value propositions, section banners, and academic chapter headings. Rendered with tight letter tracking to emphasize dignified restraint.
- **Section Eyebrows:** Set exclusively in uppercase `Inter` (12px, weight 700, 0.08em tracking) in deep emerald (`#0D7E6C`), pre-announcing the content domain (e.g., "TALEEM UL ISLAM", "OPTIONAL ENRICHMENT").

### Body & Data
- **Body & Controls (`Inter`):** Drives all instructive paragraphs, feature lists, data grids, and interactive form fields. Body text is set at comfortable line heights (1.5x–1.6x) with high-legibility dark-slate ink to ensure seamless reading for extended sessions across desktop and mobile screens.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a structured 12-column responsive fluid grid with generous vertical cadence.

### Grid & Canvas Structure
- **Max Canvas Width:** 1280px container width for standard content, allowing wide breathing room against the ivory backdrop.
- **Split Hero/Lead Layout:** A balanced 7:5 or 8:4 desktop split pairing editorial copy and journey pills on the left with prominent media arches or floating interaction forms on the right.
- **Gutters & Margins:** Desktop spacing utilizes a 24px (1.5rem) column gutter and 32px (2rem) canvas margins. Mobile scales down to a 16px (1rem) gutter and 20px (1.25rem) side margins.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Major thematic transitions utilize `space-3xl` (96px), while component-internal relationships rely on `space-md` (16px) and `space-lg` (24px).

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids loud skeuomorphism or harsh multi-tier drop shadows, prioritizing surface warmth, tonal layers, and hairline organic outlines.

### Surface Elevation Principles
- **Tier 0 (Canvas Base):** The persistent warm ivory `#FBF9F4`.
- **Tier 1 (Surface Containers):** Soft warm stone `#F4EFE6` used for structured content blocks (such as assessment callouts and optional curriculum panels) with zero drop shadow, separated only by background value shifts.
- **Tier 2 (Elevated Utility Panels):** White `#FFFFFF` cards (e.g., the assessment booking card) floating subtly over the ivory base. Supported by a delicate, tinted ambient shadow: `box-shadow: 0 10px 30px -10px rgba(7, 32, 38, 0.06), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(7, 32, 38, 0.04)`.
- **Ghost Outlines:** Structural dividers and card perimeters use a 1px solid border (`#E8E2D5`), imparting crisp scholastic definition without clutter.
- **Dark Ground:** High-contrast anchor bands (footer/header) utilize deep obsidian slate (`#072026`) with interior hairline borders of `#163840` for discreet section dividers.

## Shapes

The design system incorporates intentional geometry balancing modern friendliness with classical restraint:

- **Component Standard (`rounded-md` / 8px - 10px):** Form fields, input wrappers, step cards, and standard containers maintain an 8px radius.
- **Card Containers (`rounded-lg` / 16px - 20px):** Large curriculum containers, assessment sidecars, and journey callouts employ a 16px radius.
- **Pill Badges (`rounded-full` / 9999px):** All categorical tags, step tags, journey sequence markers, and filter chips use complete pill rounding to provide tactile, approachable touch targets.
- **Hero Arch Framing:** Featured imagery can use a classical architectural arch radius (flat bottom with fully arched top: `border-radius: 200px 200px 16px 16px`) echoing traditional scholastic arches.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Filled emerald spruce (`#0D7E6C`) with crisp white label text, `rounded-md` (8px radius) or `rounded-full`, 12px 24px padding. Hover transitions to `#0F937E` with a slight luminance boost.
- **Secondary / Ghost CTA:** Clean transparent or ivory surface with a 1px border of `#0D7E6C` or `#E8E2D5`, text rendered in `#072026`. On hover, backgrounds gently tint with `#E9F5F3`.

### Badge Pills & Course Flow
- **Curriculum Step Pills:** Rendered as horizontal pill sequences connected by subtle directional chevrons (`>`). Styled with white or soft ivory background, `#E8E2D5` border, and 14px text accompanied by micro iconography (e.g., book, crescent, compass).
- **Domain Eyebrows:** Text-only or enclosed micro-pills with tracking and bold weight (`#0D7E6C`).

### Interactive Form Cards
- **Floating Assessment Form:** Pure white background, 16px border-radius, hairline `#E8E2D5` border, padded with 24px to 32px of interior space. 
- **Form Inputs:** 42px height, 1px border (`#D1D5DB`), 8px border radius, placeholder in muted slate (`#9CA3AF`). Focus states display a crisp emerald ring (`#0D7E6C`) with a 2px outer glow (`rgba(13, 126, 108, 0.15)`).

### Step Progression Cards
- **Numbered Journey Steps (01, 02, 03):** 3-column card sequences framed in `#E8E2D5` with clear numerical prefixes, micro teal iconography in subtle rounded badge enclosures, bold titles, and concise descriptive body text.

### Accordions & Expandable FAQs
- **Question Rows:** Minimalist horizontal rows bounded by single top/bottom borders (`#E8E2D5`). The question is set in `Playfair Display` (20px) or `Inter` semi-bold, paired with a delicate teal `+` / `−` accordion glyph.
